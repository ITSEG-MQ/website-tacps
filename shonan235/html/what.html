What is a Shonan Meeting?

 

Shonan Meetings (湘南会議) are internationally recognized, invitation-based research seminars organized by the National Institute of Informatics (NII), Japan. They are designed to bring together leading researchers from around the world to discuss emerging research challenges, exchange ideas across disciplines, and develop long-term research agendas.

 

Unlike conventional conferences, Shonan Meetings emphasize deep discussion, collaboration, and agenda-setting, rather than paper presentation. Participants are selected to ensure diversity of expertise and perspectives, and meetings typically lead to influential vision papers, collaborative projects, and sustained international research networks.
