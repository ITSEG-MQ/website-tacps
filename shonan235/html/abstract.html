Meeting Abstract

 

Learning-enabled cyber-physical systems (CPS) are increasingly deployed in safety-critical domains such as autonomous driving, robotics, and unmanned aerial systems. While foundation models and large language models (LLMs) offer unprecedented capabilities for perception, reasoning, and automation, they also introduce significant challenges for testing, verification, and trust assurance.

 

This seminar focuses on how foundation models can be systematically leveraged to support the synthesis, safety testing, and formal verification of learning-enabled CPS. Topics include scenario and data generation, specification inference, AI-assisted verification workflows, correctness guarantees, and the verification of foundation models themselves. By bringing together experts from AI, formal methods, software engineering, and CPS, the seminar aims to establish a shared research agenda and identify principled paths toward trustworthy, deployable intelligent systems.

 

 

Technical Themes

 

The seminar is organized around three tightly connected technical themes:

 

  • Theme 1: latest trends in foundation models for autonomous systems. Trends in foundation models for autonomous systems, their capabilities and limitations, and system-level implications for design, deployment, and assurance.

    •  

  • Theme 2: Foundation Models for Safety Testing. Use of foundation models for scenario generation, corner-case discovery, simulation-based testing, data synthesis, and evaluation pipelines.

    •  

  • Theme 3: Foundation Models for Formal Verification. Applying foundation models to assist formal verification, including specification generation, model construction, verification oracles, and verification of foundation models themselves.
